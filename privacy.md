Uma Workshop Assistant — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. About this bot

Uma Workshop Assistant is a Discord bot made for the Uma Workshop Academy server.

2. Information the bot may process

Depending on its enabled features, the bot may process:

- Discord user IDs and server IDs.
- Messages containing bot commands.
- Message content when configured word detection is enabled.
- Other information needed for the bot's features to work.

3. How information is used

Information may be processed to:

- Respond to dad joke commands.
- Provide automotive facts.
- Detect configured words when word detection is enabled.
- Operate and maintain the bot.

4. Word detection

If enabled, the bot may inspect messages in configured server channels to identify words selected by server administrators.

The feature is intended to notify moderators. It does not automatically punish users.

5. Third-party services

The bot uses Discord and BotGhost. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data sharing

We do not intentionally sell personal information. Information may be processed by the third-party services required to operate the bot.

7. Security

Reasonable steps are taken to protect bot credentials and information. However, no online service can guarantee perfect security.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the administrators of the Uma Workshop Academy Discord server.
